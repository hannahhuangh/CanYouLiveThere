Reflection

So... which offer is better?

The data proves that "better" doesn't mean "higher paying." Allan’s real decision depends on how much of his paycheck is taken by local rent, and what trade-offs he is willing to accept.

There is no single right answer. San Diego’s higher costs might be worth it for the lifestyle, however Philadelphia might win for anyone prioritizing immediate savings and financial flexibility. Affordability is not just about finding the highest salary — it is about asking: How much is a city's environment worth to you, and what are you willing to give up to live there?
